Confirmed design problem
The admin previously sent every stock/availability edit through a site-wide
Framer publication. The existing storefront and checkout already obtain size
availability and stock from the backend. A failed CMS publication also paused
the entire product family, rather than just the intended size.
This identifies an unnecessary dependency; it does NOT prove the exact SDK
cause of the earlier live CMS publication failure.

Local implementation
- A strict content comparison recognizes only quantity, counted-stock state,
  colour availability and size availability as backend-owned updates.
- These changes commit catalogue publication and inventory in one transaction,
  with the existing revision, inventory-version and ownership guards.
- Names, prices, descriptions, photos, identities, product/colour/size ordering
  and all other content changes still require the existing Framer connection.
- CMS mappings/hashes are retained; stock updates do not claim a CMS publish.
- The admin accepts a confirmed inventory response and shows a short, accurate
  success message without changing the layout or styling.
- Errors before any CMS write use safe messages and do not pause the product.
- Any attempted CMS mutation with an uncertain result remains needs_review.
- Expired operations cannot start subsequent guarded CMS writes or publishing.
- Existing needs_review records are NOT silently unlocked or overwritten.
