Draft stock recovery review - 24 October 2026

Status: implemented and built locally for review. Not pushed or deployed.

Recovery behaviour
- Refresh untouched draft quantities from current inventory without losing saved content or availability edits.
- Preserve deliberately edited quantities, requiring explicit physical-stock confirmation if inventory changed in the meantime.
- Treat older drafts without a stock baseline conservatively: use current quantities and request review of the saved proposal.
- Reject a stale save if another purchase happens after review. Confirmation does not bypass the inventory version check.
- Keep publishing and needs-review records locked; recovery does not silently unlock an uncertain publication.
- Recovery reads do not persist changes. Saving a reviewed draft does not publish or change live inventory.
