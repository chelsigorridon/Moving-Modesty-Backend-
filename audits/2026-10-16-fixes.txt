MOVING MODESTY — APPROVED WEBSITE AUDIT FIXES
16 October 2026

Scope: the six fixes explicitly requested after the website audit.
Live Framer release: 812b27618.

1. About Us mobile clipping
   Made the story body and supporting paragraph fill their bounded parent,
   with zero minimum width and automatic height. Preserved the desktop reading
   width, fonts, content and section order. Verified readable live text at
   320 and 390 px; tablet/desktop width checks also completed.

2. Admin Products mobile list
   Small-screen rows now use a thumbnail + flexible text column, with the Edit
   action on a separate full row. At 390 px, the title has about 192 px of usable
   width instead of less than 1 px; both product rows are about 201 px tall
   instead of over 1,050 px. Desktop layout is retained.

3. Free-delivery copy
   Corrected all nine Products CMS records from R1,500 to R1,200. The popup now
   explicitly says "Free delivery on orders of R1,200 or more."
   Both Neon catalogue draft/published documents and their CMS fingerprints
   were updated transactionally with exact old-version guards. Both products
   are revision 4/published revision 4, idle. No stock or price changed.
   Verified the popup and a customer-facing Delivery & returns accordion live.

4. Product Preview stock
   Shared variantAvailable() includes uncounted stock (stockVerified:false),
   while still respecting the colour/size availability switches and confirmed
   zero stock. Live Hawa Preview enables seven size/colour choices and disables
   only Grey Large, labelled "Out of stock". No product edit was saved during
   the preview check. Regenerated the embedded Framer admin from shared source.

5. Legacy cart merging
   Added shared canonical SKU normalization, mechanically bundled into the
   purchase, cart and checkout-summary single-file Framer components. Legacy
   entries merge with current entries without losing quantity; different sizes
   and colours stay separate. Unknown/invalid lines remain visible for existing
   validation instead of being silently discarded. Add checks aggregate stock
   and the ten-per-variant limit. Tests cover mixed legacy/current identities,
   idempotence, unknown products, quantity preservation and aggregate limits.
   Live test: original Lavender quantity four + one Add produced one line at
   quantity five/R2,100. Decreased back to the original quantity four/R1,680.
   No checkout order, email, payment or courier booking was created.

6. Button-label contrast
   Primary storefront actions now use the darker existing olive family:
   #536047 with #EAE6E3 labels, approximately 5.40:1 contrast. Updated explicit
   canvas overrides, generated checkout navigation, contact/newsletter actions,
   product preview and shared native Button variants. Admin main actions also
   use the darker fill. Decorative sage accents and the overall layout remain.
   Computed live Add, Join and Send colours were checked, not just defaults.
